Privacy Policy — Mazda MX-30 Companion

Effective date: November 18, 2026 • Service: Mazda MX-30 Companion

1. Data We Collect

  • Account & Identity: Google Account email address, display name, and Google Subject ID (`sub`) used strictly for authentication.
  • Passkeys (WebAuthn): Public key credentials stored for passwordless sign-in.
  • Vehicle & Location Data: MyMazda OAuth2 tokens, vehicle telemetry (battery SoC, range, climate, lock/door/window status, tire pressures), GPS coordinates (for parked map, local weather, regional public/bank holidays, and saved places), schedules, and recorded trip/charge logs.

2. Encryption & Zero Tracking

All user profiles, vehicle telemetry, OAuth tokens, saved places, and schedules are stored in an isolated per-user vault encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and transmitted exclusively over HTTPS/TLS. No third-party ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs are used.

3. Data Export & Account Deletion

You can export your data (CSV/JSON) or permanently delete your account and all encrypted data at any time from the web portal or see Delete your account.

4. Support & GDPR Contact

Questions, data requests, or support: mx30Support@gmail.com
