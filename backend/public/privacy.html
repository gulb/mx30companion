Privacy Policy — Mazda MX-30 Companion

Effective date: November 18, 2026 • Service URL: https://mx30.g-family.uk

1. Data We Collect

  • Google Account Identity: Email address, display name, and Google subject ID used strictly for authentication.
  • Passkeys (WebAuthn): Public key credentials stored for passwordless sign-in.
  • Vehicle & Location Data: MyMazda OAuth2 tokens, vehicle telemetry (battery SoC, range, climate, lock/door status, tire pressures), saved places, schedules, and recorded trip/charge logs.

2. Encryption & Storage

All user profiles, vehicle telemetry, OAuth tokens, and schedules are stored in an isolated per-user vault encrypted with AES-256-GCM. No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs are used.

3. Data Control & Account Deletion

You can export your data (JSON/CSV) or permanently delete your account and all encrypted data at any time directly inside the web portal using the Delete Account button.

4. Contact

For support or data inquiries, contact the server administrator at mx30.g-family.uk.
