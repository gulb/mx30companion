Delete Your Account — Mazda MX-30 Companion

You can permanently delete your Mazda MX-30 Companion account and all associated encrypted data at any time.

Option 1: Instant Self-Service Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Sign in to the Web Portal with your Google Account or Passkey.
  2. Scroll to the Connect Your Mazda Car (OAuth2) card and click Delete Account.
  3. Confirm deletion. All data is erased immediately and cannot be recovered.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account, email mx30Support@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject Delete MX-30 Companion Account. Your account and all encrypted data will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.

What is deleted:
  • Google Account identity link (email, name, Google ID)
  • Registered WebAuthn Passkeys and active sessions
  • MyMazda OAuth2 tokens, saved places, schedules, trip/charge logs, and timeline history
